# AHAD MOTORS 🚗💨
> "Drive Your Dream" - Pakistan's Premier Automotive Marketplace

AHAD MOTORS is a modern, responsive, client-side car buying and selling web platform designed specifically for the Pakistani automotive market. Explore verified vehicle listings, detailed condition matrices, real-time EMI loan calculation, seller live chat simulations, and free instant ad posting.

---

## ✨ Features

- 🔍 **Dynamic Search & Filtering:** Filter by Make, Model, Price Range, Year, Fuel Type, Transmission, City, and Condition.
- ⚡ **Instant Sorting:** Sort listings by Price (Low to High / High to Low), Newest Year, and Lowest Mileage.
- 🖼️ **Multi-Angle Gallery Modal:** High-resolution multi-view image gallery with thumbnails and next/previous controls.
- 📋 **Vehicle Condition Transparency:** Dedicated specs for accident history, paint touch-ups, owner count, and feature badges.
- 💬 **Live Seller Chat Simulator:** Instant simulated chat interface with quick inquiry chips (*"Is the car still available?", "Can I inspect the car?"*).
- 📱 **Floating WhatsApp Direct Support:** One-click WhatsApp link pre-filled with listing inquiries.
- 📝 **"Sell Your Car" Ad Submission:** Free ad creation form with instant local inventory addition and notification toasts.
- 💰 **Auto Loan / Financing Calculator:** Real-time monthly installment (EMI) calculator based on KIBOR rates and tenure.
- ❤️ **Saved Favorites:** Persistent `localStorage` favorite system with navbar counter and quick-view toggle.
- 📱 **100% Mobile Responsive:** Optimized layout with a mobile drawer menu and responsive card grids.
